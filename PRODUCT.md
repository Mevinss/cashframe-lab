# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Confirmed implementation plan: plain HTML, CSS and browser JavaScript, served by FastAPI. No external runtime frontend services.

## Users

Bank video reviewers checking a cash operation on a laptop; jury evaluating the exact asynchronous API.

## Product Purpose

Upload a new video and inspect local-model signals with source timestamps and original evidence. A human confirms actions and applicable rules.

## Operating Context

Three states: upload, real processing stages, results with video and timeline. Russian primary language; accessible keyboard navigation and narrow-screen panel stacking.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Source videos remain local. No cloud video AI, facial identification or generated evidence. Binary weak labels exist; specific event types and legal matches remain unconfirmed. No percentages presented as calibrated confidence. No claim of guaranteed safety.

## Brand Commitments

User provided forte.kz reference: light working surfaces, graphite text, burgundy/raspberry primary actions, moderate corners and thin borders. Functional headings, no invented brand name, logo or slogan. No marketing hero or decorative charts.

## Evidence on Hand

Aggregate audit and executable model pipeline. No verified type taxonomy, human evidence relevance assessment or novel-angle benchmark. Do not fabricate these.

## Product Principles

Lead with video and evidence. Preserve source timestamps. Explain unknowns. Separate observation, model signal and possible rule inconsistency. Technical failures stay explicit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Visible keyboard focus, labeled inputs, text accompanying priority colors, readable contrast, Russian strings stored separately for future Kazakh localization.
