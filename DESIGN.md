# Visual contract — approved 18 October 2026

The user's screenshots pin burgundy/magenta and cyan dimensional materials, supported by neutral working surfaces. No official bank logo, camera glyph, CashFrame name or Datathon subtitle appears in the product. This describes the implemented interface; it is not an official bank brand guide.

## Overview

A large saturated banner pairs a naturally wrapping heading and stationary upload action with the approved CSS 3D composition of a video viewer, reels and an evidence sheet. The sculpture remains fixed in its approved pose, with no drag, pointer tilt, keyboard movement, reset action or tab stop. Vertical touch scrolling remains native. Geometry has no pretend playback controls or model measurements.

Three short copy panels explain upload, local model signals and human review with PDF export. Only the copy slides; the approved burgundy/cyan sculpture and upload action stay stationary. Pagination and previous/next remain available with arrow, Home and End keyboard navigation in the controls. Per the latest explicit user direction, rotation changes panels every five seconds throughout the visible overview, including during hover or focus. There is no pause control. Hidden pages, offscreen banners and views other than overview suspend the timer to avoid background work. Reduced motion retains the same five-second changes with instant transitions. Broader text columns and full localized heading phrases allow natural wrapping in Russian, Kazakh and English.

## Review workspace

Exact localized API verdict, source file/duration, export actions, source video and timeline, event list, original JPEG evidence and event-specific normative panel. Unknown timing suppresses zero-second incident markers and labels images as context. Human observation, model proposal and legal applicability have separate states. New results open at the top; language changes preserve position and playback.

The result heading has its own row. A dedicated toolbar beneath it gives PDF export the primary weight, evidence passport secondary weight, and new video a tertiary text action. The toolbar stays inline on desktop and wraps naturally on narrow screens. Programmatic heading focus avoids a full-width outline; keyboard focus retains an underline. Human action entry offers optional localized catalog suggestions and always permits a reviewer's own wording, including when the catalog is unavailable. Suggestions describe observation categories, not trained model predictions.

## Themes and content

Light and dark use semantic colors on surfaces, fields, menus, dialogs, timelines and status panels; source images are never inverted. Russian, Kazakh and English cover labels, states, accessibility names and errors. Source records and official document text remain in their original language with an explicit notice. Percentages are measured stage work, never model confidence.

## Responsive behavior

Wide screens retain navigation and a two-column review workspace. Narrow screens use a compact rail and stacked panels; the hero remains large with its composition below the action. Contrast, visible focus, tabular times and native controls matter more than decorative detail. Synthetic captures at 1366 and 390 px verify no horizontal overflow in all three locales and both themes.

## Signature interaction and finish evidence

Five-second explanatory slides with keyboard navigation alongside a fixed decorative sculpture. Evidence and controls remain stationary. Final synthetic captures and browser checks are saved under local/audit/sprint-final-* and sprint-browser-checks.json; source/DOM regressions are in tests/frontend.test.cjs.
